# Research: The GIF patent fight, and how it gave us PNG

- Slug: the-gif-patent-fight
- Key phrase people search for: gif patent unisys
- One-sentence summary of the true story: The compression inside every GIF was patented by Unisys; when it started charging for it in the mid-1990s, programmers responded by inventing PNG and, in 1999, by staging a "Burn All GIFs Day", until the patents expired in 2003–2004.

All sources accessed 2026-10-12.
