# Research: Who invented the GIF? The CompuServe engineer behind the format

- Slug: who-invented-the-gif
- Key phrase people search for: who invented the gif
- One-sentence summary of the true story: In 1987 Steve Wilhite, an engineer at the online service CompuServe, created the GIF so color pictures could be shared efficiently over the slow modem connections of the time; animation came later and was never the point.

All sources accessed 2026-10-12.
