Photos on X can be saved with a right-click or a long press. GIFs can't, and that isn't a bug.

## Why there's no Save button

When someone uploads a GIF, X converts it into a short looping video and throws the .gif away. What plays in your feed with the GIF badge is an MP4. Your browser treats it as a video inside the page, not as an image you can save.

## How to get it as a real GIF

1. On the post, tap **Share** and choose **Copy link**.
2. Open [GifSuite](/) and paste the link.
3. Click **Grab GIF**.
4. Pick a quality and download it as a **GIF**.

GifSuite finds the video behind the post and converts it back into a .gif file.

## Which download should you pick?

- **GIF** works everywhere an image does: email, documents, chats, forums.
- **MP4** is the original file from X. It's the smallest and the sharpest, and the best choice for WhatsApp, Instagram or posting again.
- **Low, Medium or High** trades file size against quality. Medium suits most uses; choose Low for a quick send.

## If you just want to repost it on X

Skip the download. After grabbing the GIF, use **Post on X**. Your post plays the original GIF and credits the account that posted it, with nothing to upload.
