You found the perfect GIF on Giphy or Tenor, but saving it gets you a WebP image, a video your phone won't share as a GIF, or nothing at all. GIF sites are built for sharing a link, not for handing you a file.

## Why saving a GIF from Giphy or Tenor is awkward

Most GIF sites don't show you a .gif at all. To load quickly, they play a small video or a WebP animation, and switch formats depending on your browser. A right-click often saves one of those, and many apps don't treat it as a GIF.

## How to download it as a real GIF

1. Open the GIF's own page on Giphy or Tenor and copy the link from your browser's address bar. A direct link to the GIF file works too.
2. Open [GifSuite](/) and paste the link.
3. Click **Grab GIF**.
4. Pick a format and quality, and download.

GifSuite reads the GIF from the page, saves it as a clean copy and gives it its own page, so you can download it again or share it later.

## GIF, MP4 or WebM?

- **GIF** plays everywhere an image does: email, documents, chats and forums.
- **MP4** is the smallest and sharpest, and the best choice for WhatsApp, Instagram or posting on X.
- **WebM** is a small video format that suits websites.

If your file comes out too big for where it's going, run it through [Compress & resize](/tools/compress) to fit a size limit. For more on the formats, read [GIF vs MP4 vs WebM vs WebP](/blog/gif-vs-mp4-vs-webm-vs-webp).

## Can I use any GIF I download?

Downloading a GIF doesn't make it yours. Most belong to someone, so use them the way the creator and the site's own terms allow.
