Posting a GIF on X is simple until the upload fails or the result looks worse than your file. Both come down to two facts.

## The size limits

At the time of writing, X's help pages give these limits for animated GIFs:

- **15 MB** when you post from the website
- **5 MB** when you post from the mobile apps

A GIF made from a few seconds of video easily passes 15 MB. If X rejects yours, it's almost always the file size.

## X turns your GIF into a video

X doesn't keep the .gif file. It converts it to a small looping MP4 video and shows that with a GIF badge. This is why saving a GIF from X gives you a video, and why a GIF can look slightly different after posting.

It also means extra detail in your file is wasted. A 1080px-wide, 256-color GIF is converted anyway, so a smaller upload usually looks the same once posted.

## Getting yours to fit

1. **Trim it** to the part that matters. Length is the biggest factor in size.
2. **Resize it** to about 480px wide. Feeds rarely show it larger.
3. **Compress it** to the limit for where you're posting from.

[Compress & resize](/tools/compress) has presets for both limits. Choose **X** for the website or **5 MB** for the apps, and it shrinks the GIF until it fits.

## Reposting a GIF that's already on X

If the GIF you want is in someone else's post, you don't need to download and re-upload it. [Grab it](/) with GifSuite and use **Post on X**: your post plays the original GIF and credits the person who posted it.
